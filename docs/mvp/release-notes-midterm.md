What’s included in the midterm build
  - Game starts with few click and quickly
   - Space ship catching energy and avoiding asteroids
   - Score increasing as energy is caught and life decreased as asteroids is caught
   - Space ship can move left and right and stay with in the frame
   - Single player game with no online integration
